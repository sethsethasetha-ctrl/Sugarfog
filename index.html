About the game

Glimbrule: Sugar Siege is a landscape 2D tower defense game for iPhone and iPad. Build a new defense every run across five candy worlds and twenty campaign stages.

  • Twelve towers with different attack, control, support, and economy roles.
  • Twenty-three enemy types and eight bosses with distinct abilities.
  • Relics, core skills, debris clearing, campaign progression, and Endless mode.
  • Offline play with local progress on the device.
